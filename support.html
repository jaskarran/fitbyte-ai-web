Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI nutrition tracking work?
FitByte AI uses advanced computer vision and natural language processing to identify foods from photos or text descriptions. It considers portion sizes and regional variations to provide accurate nutritional data.
Can I use FitByte AI offline?
Yes! FitByte AI is designed with an offline-first approach. You can log your meals and activities without an internet connection, and they will sync once you're back online.
Is my health data secure?
Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data. Your personal health information is never shared with third parties without your explicit consent.
